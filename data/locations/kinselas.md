Kinselas is a heritage-listed venue in Darlinghurst, originally built as a funeral parlour and chapel of rest in 1919. It became one of Sydney's most prominent gay-friendly nightclubs in the 1980s and 1990s, located at 383 Bourke Street in the heart of the Oxford Street LGBTIQ+ precinct.

In the early hours of 23 December 1993, Crispin Dye was found severely injured in the laneway behind the building — Little Oxford Street, at the intersection with Campbell Street. He had been drinking at various hotels in and around Oxford Street through the night and was last seen heavily intoxicated in Oxford Street at around 4:00am.

Dye was transported to St Vincent's Hospital and later St George Hospital, where he died on 25 December 1993. No person has ever been charged with his death.
