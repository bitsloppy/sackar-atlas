Alison Park in Randwick was a known gay beat in the 1980s. The park sits adjacent to Alison Road and was surrounded by residential boarding houses.

At the time of Raymond Keam's murder in January 1987, Stanley Early (then known as "Spider Sutton") lived in a boarding house directly opposite the park. Evidence at trial established that Early and a group of men would "set upon" men in the park late at night because they believed them to be gay.

Raymond Keam was found face up in the grass in Alison Park on the morning of 13 January 1987. He died from a brain haemorrhage sustained in the attack.

The park was not the only site of violence against gay men in the area — Strike Force Taradale (2002) identified links between Raymond Keam's murder and a pattern of attacks by groups of youths targeting men perceived to be gay across Bondi, Marks Park, Alexandria, and other eastern suburbs beats.
