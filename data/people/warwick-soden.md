Coroner Warwick Graeme Soden presided over the coronial inquest into the death of Bill Rooney, held on 24 October 1986 and 15 May 1987. He delivered an open finding on 15 May 1987, determining that Rooney died of head injuries sustained on 14 February 1986 but that the evidence did not enable him to say whether those injuries were received accidentally or otherwise.

This open finding was later confirmed as consistent with the conclusions of the Sackar Inquiry.
