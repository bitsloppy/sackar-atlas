Detective Sergeant Geoffrey Roy Knight was the Officer in Charge (OIC) of Strike Force Barcoo, the original police investigation into the death of Crispin Dye in 1993. He prepared the summary for the 1995 inquest and gave evidence before State Coroner Hand.
