Raymond Frederick Keam was 43 years old when he was killed in Alison Park, Randwick, on the morning of 13 January 1987. He was a married man and father; his son was named Dane.

Police alleged the murder was a gay-hate crime motivated by a false perception of Keam's sexual orientation. Keam was attacked by Stanley Bruce Early (then known as Stanley "Spider" Sutton), who had been part of a group that targeted men they believed to be gay in Alison Park — a known gay beat. Early was convicted of murder in June 2023 and sentenced to a maximum of 22 years by Justice Dina Yehia of the NSW Supreme Court.
