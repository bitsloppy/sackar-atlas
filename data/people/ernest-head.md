Ernest Allan Head was 44 years old when he was killed in his apartment on Grosvenor Crescent, Summer Hill, on the evening of 17 June 1976. Born in Malaysia in 1932, he had emigrated to Australia in 1960 and worked as a clerk with the Corporate Affairs Commission. Friends described him as quiet, kind, and immaculate in his personal appearance.

He was a gay man who had been in a long-term relationship and had disclosed his sexuality to his brother, Arthur Head. Arthur Head is now deceased.

In 2023, the Sackar Inquiry caused palm prints from the 1976 crime scene to be re-examined using V11 NAFIS technology. A palm print in blood on the kitchen wall above Head's body was matched to Engin Simsek — a man whose fingerprints had been in police files since 1980. Simsek died in Türkiye in 1999. No criminal proceedings are now possible.
