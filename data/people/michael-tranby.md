Constable Michael Tranby was the officer in charge (OIC) of the original police investigation into the death of Bill Rooney, based at Wollongong Police Station. His investigation in 1986 has since been found by the Sackar Inquiry to have been inadequate in multiple respects.

The Inquiry wrote to Tranby twice in 2023 (24 August and 19 September), enclosing written submissions. He did not respond.
