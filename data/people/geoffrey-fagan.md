Detective Sergeant Geoffrey William Fagan was the Officer in Charge (OIC) of the original police investigation into the death of Ernest Head in 1976, conducted by Ashfield Police Station with the assistance of the Special Crime Squad. Sackar found this investigation to be "extensive and thorough."

Detective Sergeant Fagan is now deceased, confirmed via interagency cooperation by the Sackar Inquiry.
