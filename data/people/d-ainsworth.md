Detective Inspector Ainsworth (first name unknown from available SCOI records — referred to only as "Inspector D Ainsworth" in exhibit references) was a Wollongong detective assigned to investigate most of the twelve attacks attributed to Mark Scerri between 1986 and 1989.

Although not formally assigned to the original Rooney investigation, Ainsworth heard the callout on 14 February 1986, attended the scene the following day, and formed the view that it was most unlikely Rooney had fallen. He suspected Rooney had been taken to the spot and dropped on his head.

During his investigation of the Scerri attacks, Ainsworth formed the view that Rooney was likely Scerri's first victim. He wrote to the ODPP on 4 July 1990 outlining available evidence and requesting a substantial reward. He referred the Rooney case to the ODPP for consideration of murder charges on 28 March 1991 and again on 24 March 1993. Both referrals were declined for insufficient evidence.

The Inquiry spoke with Ainsworth about his work in connection with the investigation of the Rooney case.
