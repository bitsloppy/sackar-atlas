Crispin Wilson Dye was 41 years old when he was killed in Darlinghurst on Christmas morning 1993. A music publisher and musician, he lived mostly in Cairns, Queensland, and was visiting Sydney at the time of his death. Contemporary press described him as having managed AC/DC.

Dye was found in the laneway behind Kinselas nightclub, Darlinghurst, in the early hours of 23 December 1993, with severe head injuries. He died two days later at St George Hospital, Kogarah. No person has ever been charged with his death. In 2023, forensic re-testing of his clothing — conducted at the direction of the Sackar Inquiry — discovered a previously unknown DNA profile ("Unknown Male B") from a hair found on his shirt.
