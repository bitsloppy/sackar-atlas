Stanley Bruce Early was convicted of the murder of Raymond Keam on 7 June 2023 in the NSW Supreme Court, following a jury trial. On 20 October 2023, Justice Dina Yehia sentenced him — then aged 77 — to a maximum of 22 years in prison.

At the time of the murder in January 1987, Early went by the name Stanley "Spider" Sutton and lived in a boarding house directly opposite Alison Park, Randwick. Evidence at trial established that he had been part of a group who "set upon" men in the park late at night because they believed them to be gay. He had made homophobic remarks to neighbours and bragged about violence against men he perceived to be homosexual.

Early was arrested in August 2021, more than 34 years after the murder. His identification followed publicity generated by Strike Force Parrabell's community outreach component.
