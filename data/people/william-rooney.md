Bill Rooney was 35 years old when he died on 20 February 1986. He lived in Gwynneville, a suburb of Wollongong, with his de facto partner Wayne Davis.

An openly gay man, Bill had a reputation among friends as someone who could drink a fair amount and still remain steady on his feet. He was found in a semi-conscious state in Crown Lane, Wollongong, on the morning of 14 February 1986, suffering from catastrophic head injuries. He died six days later at Wollongong Hospital.

His de facto partner Wayne Davis, and his aunt Irene Eipeldauer (whom the Inquiry attempted to contact), were his closest known family. Davis identified Bill to police on the day he was found. Davis is now deceased.

The circumstances of Bill's death — whether assault or accidental fall — remain undetermined. No one has ever been charged in connection with his death.
